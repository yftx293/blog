# Design notes

This site is a personal blog for Yu's writing on AI agents, coding agents, and engineering practice. The interface keeps long articles readable and makes the writing easy to find.

## Layout

- The homepage introduces the author, links to current writing, and offers a small interactive terminal.
- Article pages use a comfortable reading width, clear heading levels, and a table of contents for longer posts.
- Navigation stays short: Blog, Projects, Links, About, and Contact.
- Chinese is the primary language. English pages explain the site and link to available writing.

## Type and color

Body text favors legibility across Chinese and Latin characters. Headings, body text, code, and muted metadata have distinct visual roles. The palette is restrained, with enough contrast in light and dark modes.

## Motion

The optional homepage intro can be skipped and respects reduced motion. Content remains accessible without animation or JavaScript.

## Content

Pages should describe Yu's actual work and contact channels. New articles live under `src/content/blog/`. Images and links should be checked before publication.
